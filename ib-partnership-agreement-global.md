These Independent Partnership Agreement — Global Terms (the "Agreement") are entered into between **Kohle Capital Markets Limited** (the "Company"), a company incorporated in the Republic of Mauritius (Company Registration No. C153797 C1/GBL), holding an Investment Dealer (Full Service Dealer, Excluding Underwriting) Licence (Licence No. **C117022600**, SEC-2.1B) and a Global Business Licence (FS-4.1) issued by the **Financial Services Commission of Mauritius (FSC Mauritius)** under the Financial Services Act 2007 and the Securities Act 2005, with its registered office at The Cyberati Lounge, C/o Credentia International Management Ltd, Ground Floor, The Catalyst, Silicon Avenue, 40 Cybercity, 72201 Ebène, Republic of Mauritius — and the **Partner** (as identified and acknowledged in the execution mechanism applicable to this Agreement).

The Company operates trading and financial services under the **KCM Trade** brand. The Partner desires to act as an Introducing Broker of the Company within the **Designated Territory** as defined in Clause 1 of this Agreement, in accordance with the terms and conditions set out herein.

> **Territorial Scope:** This Agreement governs introducing broker activities conducted in jurisdictions outside the African continent. Partners operating exclusively within Africa must enter into the separate [Independent Partnership Agreement — Introducing Broker (Africa)]({{ '/ib-partnership-agreement.html' | relative_url }}). Where a Partner operates in both Africa and other regions, two separate agreements are required, one for each territory.

> **Regulatory Notice:** The Company holds a Global Business Licence and Investment Dealer Licence issued by the FSC Mauritius, which authorise it to conduct regulated investment dealing activities with clients on a global basis from Mauritius. The Company does not represent that its Mauritius authorisation constitutes a licence, registration, or authorisation in any other jurisdiction. Partners are solely responsible for determining whether their IB activities in any given jurisdiction require a local licence, registration, or regulatory authorisation, and for obtaining and maintaining any such authorisation.

This Agreement shall be read and construed together with the Company's **Compensation Schedule** (the "Compensation Schedule"), which sets out the applicable commission, rebate, and remuneration structure and is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. In the event of any inconsistency between this Agreement and the Compensation Schedule on matters of commercial remuneration only, the Compensation Schedule shall prevail. On all other matters — including compliance, prohibited conduct, jurisdictional restrictions, data protection, confidentiality, intellectual property, non-circumvention, and brand protection — this Agreement prevails.

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

**"Introduced Client"** means any individual or entity referred to the Company by the Partner who successfully registers for and activates a trading account with the Company as a direct result of the Partner's introduction.

**"Services"** means the trading platform, accounts, tools, and related financial services offered by the Company under the KCM Trade brand, as more fully described in the Company's [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms-of-service.html' | relative_url }}).

**"Compensation Schedule"** means the separate written schedule specifying the Partner's applicable commission rates, rebates, and remuneration structure, as executed between the Parties and amended from time to time in accordance with Clause 7.2.

**"Confidential Information"** means all information disclosed by either Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked as confidential, or that a reasonable person would consider to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, including but not limited to commercial terms, commission structures, client data, business strategies, technical information, and marketing plans. The Compensation Schedule and all commission and rebate terms constitute Confidential Information of the Company.

**"KCM IP"** means the KCM Trade brand name, trade marks, logos, domain names, design assets, approved marketing templates, and all other intellectual property owned by or licensed to the Company in connection with the KCM Trade brand.

**"Applicable Law"** means all applicable laws, regulations, rules, guidance, orders, and binding regulatory requirements in force from time to time in any jurisdiction relevant to the activities carried out under this Agreement, including but not limited to the laws of Mauritius, the laws of the jurisdiction(s) in which the Partner operates or is incorporated, applicable data protection legislation, AML/CTF legislation, securities and financial services regulation, and consumer protection law.

**"IB Platform"** means the Company's back-office and partner management system through which the Partner manages referrals, monitors commissions, generates promotional materials, and accesses partner tools.

**"Competing Provider"** means any entity that provides, markets, or facilitates retail or professional access to foreign exchange (forex), contracts for difference (CFDs), margin trading, leveraged derivatives, or substantially similar financial products or services in competition with the Company.

**"Designated Territory"** means all jurisdictions globally excluding the African continent, and further excluding the **Restricted Jurisdictions** listed in Clause 3.2 of this Agreement. The Designated Territory is subject to amendment by the Company at any time in response to changes in Applicable Law, regulatory requirements, or sanctions obligations.

**"Restricted Jurisdictions"** means jurisdictions in which the Company does not accept clients or in which IB activities are prohibited, as set out in Clause 3.2 and the Company's [Jurisdictional Availability]({{ '/jurisdictional-availability.html' | relative_url }}) and [Sanctions Policy]({{ '/sanctions-policy.html' | relative_url }}).

**"Local Regulatory Authorisation"** means any licence, registration, authorisation, exemption, or approval required under the laws of a particular jurisdiction for the Partner to lawfully carry on introducing broker activities in that jurisdiction.

**"KCM Group"** means Kohle Capital Markets Limited (Mauritius), Kohle Capital Markets Pty Ltd (Australia), Kohle Technology Limited (Hong Kong), and any other entity that is from time to time a subsidiary or affiliate of Kohle Capital Markets Limited.

**"Abnormal Market Conditions"** means any circumstance in which the Company determines, acting reasonably, that pricing is materially disrupted, including as a result of exchange outages, data feed errors, illiquid conditions, or system malfunctions.

## 3. Designated Territory and Jurisdictional Compliance

### 3.1 Scope of Appointment

The Partner's appointment under this Agreement is limited to introducing clients who are resident in, or whose accounts are to be domiciled in, jurisdictions within the Designated Territory. The Partner shall not under any circumstances introduce clients from the Restricted Jurisdictions, nor shall it conduct promotional or referral activities targeting persons in those jurisdictions.

### 3.2 Restricted Jurisdictions

The following jurisdictions are expressly excluded from the Designated Territory. The Partner is strictly prohibited from introducing clients from, or conducting IB activities targeting residents of, any of the following:

| Jurisdiction | Basis for Restriction |
|---|---|
| **African continent** | Governed exclusively by the separate Africa IB Agreement |
| **United States of America** | The Company does not hold CFTC or NFA registration; US persons and US-controlled entities are excluded |
| **Australia** | Services to Australian residents are provided exclusively by Kohle Capital Markets Pty Ltd (AFSL 489437, ASIC regulated); the Mauritius entity is not authorised to serve Australian residents |
| **Canada** | Provincial securities licensing requirements apply; Canadian persons are excluded |
| **Japan** | Japan Financial Instruments and Exchange Act (FIEA) licensing requirements apply |
| **North Korea (DPRK)** | UN Security Council sanctions; OFAC SDN list |
| **Iran** | UN, US (OFAC), and EU comprehensive sanctions |
| **Myanmar** | FATF Call for Action; targeted sanctions under EU, UK, and US regimes |
| **Syria** | UN, US (OFAC), and EU comprehensive sanctions |
| **Sudan** | UN and US (OFAC) sanctions |

The Company may add further jurisdictions to the Restricted Jurisdictions list at any time by written notice to the Partner, with immediate effect where required by regulatory or sanctions obligations, or with fourteen (14) days' notice otherwise. The current and authoritative list of Restricted Jurisdictions is maintained on the Company's [Jurisdictional Availability]({{ '/jurisdictional-availability.html' | relative_url }}) and [Sanctions Policy]({{ '/sanctions-policy.html' | relative_url }}) pages, which the Partner agrees to monitor on a regular basis.

### 3.3 Local Regulatory Authorisation Obligation

The Partner is solely responsible for independently determining whether its IB activities in any particular jurisdiction within the Designated Territory require a Local Regulatory Authorisation. Without limiting the foregoing:

- (a) **Southeast Asia** — the Partner acknowledges that certain jurisdictions, including Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam, and the Philippines, impose licensing requirements on persons soliciting investment clients. The Partner shall obtain all required local authorisations before conducting IB activities in any such jurisdiction;
- (b) **Middle East (MENA)** — jurisdictions including the UAE (under DFSA, FSRA, or SCA oversight), Saudi Arabia, and Qatar impose financial promotion and intermediary regulations. The Partner shall ensure compliance with all applicable local requirements;
- (c) **Europe** — EU/EEA jurisdictions generally require investment firms and their intermediaries to be authorised under MiFID II (Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II). The Partner shall not conduct IB activities in EU/EEA jurisdictions unless it holds the requisite MiFID II authorisation or operates under an applicable exemption;
- (d) **Latin America** — securities and financial services regulation varies significantly by country. The Partner shall comply with all applicable local requirements before commencing IB activities in any Latin American jurisdiction;
- (e) **Any other jurisdiction** — the Partner shall conduct its own legal due diligence for each jurisdiction in which it proposes to operate and shall not commence IB activities in any new jurisdiction without first satisfying itself that it holds all required Local Regulatory Authorisations.

**3.4** The Partner shall promptly notify the Company in writing if it becomes aware of any regulatory requirement, change in law, or regulatory action that may affect its authority to conduct IB activities in any jurisdiction within the Designated Territory.

**3.5** The Company reserves the right to restrict the Partner's activities in any jurisdiction at any time and without prior notice where the Company reasonably determines, on the basis of legal advice or regulatory guidance, that the Partner's activities in that jurisdiction create a material legal or regulatory risk for the Company or the KCM Group.

## 8. Prohibited Trading Activities

**8.1** The Partner acknowledges and agrees that all Introduced Clients and all trading activities conducted through accounts introduced by the Partner must comply with the Company's [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms-of-service.html' | relative_url }}), trading policies, risk management standards, and regulatory requirements. The Partner shall use its best endeavours to ensure that Introduced Clients are made aware of and comply with such requirements prior to trading.

**8.2** The following activities are strictly prohibited in relation to all accounts introduced by the Partner:

- (a) exploitation of system errors, technical vulnerabilities, pricing feed malfunctions, or price latency for trading gain;
- (b) market manipulation, fraudulent acts, or fictitious transactions, including but not limited to spoofing, layering, wash trading, front-running, and momentum ignition, as further described in the Company's [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms-of-service.html' | relative_url }}) §5;
- (c) abuse of liquidity mechanisms, including disrupting orderly market functioning through high-frequency mass order placement and cancellation without genuine trading intent;
- (d) use of gap trading strategies in conjunction with maximum or near-maximum leverage positions, or any similar strategy designed to exploit overnight or weekend price gaps for the purpose of obtaining profit in a manner inconsistent with legitimate market participation;
- (e) arbitrage strategies executed during Abnormal Market Conditions;
- (f) any trading strategy characterised by a pattern of activity with no genuine commercial, directional, or hedging rationale, where the primary or predominant purpose is to extract profit from pricing anomalies, system limitations, or temporal inefficiencies;
- (g) coordinated or related-party trading activity designed to generate artificial commission flows, including but not limited to circular trading, offsetting positions across related accounts, and volume inflation without genuine risk transfer;
- (h) any act or omission that violates applicable regulatory provisions, the Company's Terms of Service, or the Company's principles of fair and legitimate trading.

**8.3** The Company reserves the right, at its sole and absolute discretion, to:

- (a) reject, suspend, block, or terminate any Introduced Client account where such account is associated with suspected or confirmed prohibited activity;
- (b) adjust, cancel, or permanently withhold commissions, rebates, and any other remuneration derived directly or indirectly from prohibited trading behaviour, including amounts already credited but not yet withdrawn;
- (c) suspend or terminate this Agreement immediately and without prior notice where the Company determines, acting reasonably, that the Partner knew, ought to have known, or was a beneficiary of prohibited activity by Introduced Clients;
- (d) report any suspected prohibited conduct to the FSC Mauritius, the Financial Intelligence Unit of Mauritius (FIU), and any other competent authority as required by Applicable Law.

**8.4** The Partner shall remain responsible for taking reasonable steps to ensure that all Introduced Clients operate in a fair, commercially legitimate, and compliant manner, and shall promptly notify the Company upon becoming aware of any suspected prohibited activity.

**8.5 Sub-IB and Agent Liability.** The Partner shall be fully liable for the acts and omissions of any sub-IB, affiliate, agent, or employee engaged by the Partner in connection with this Agreement to the same extent as if such acts and omissions were those of the Partner itself. This includes, without limitation, non-compliant promotional conduct, referral of clients from Restricted Jurisdictions, AML/KYC failures, and involvement in or facilitation of prohibited trading activities.
