## 2. Regulatory Authorisation

Kohle Capital Markets Pty Ltd holds **Australian Financial Services Licence No. 489437**, issued by the **Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC)** pursuant to Section 913B of the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth).

The AFSL authorises the licensee to:

### 2.1 General Financial Product Advice
Provide **general financial product advice** to both **retail and wholesale clients** in relation to:
- Deposit and payment products (basic deposit products and other deposit products)
- Derivatives
- Foreign exchange contracts

### 2.2 Dealing in Financial Products
**Deal in financial products** as follows:
- Issue derivatives to **wholesale clients only**
- Apply for, acquire, vary, or dispose of deposit products, derivatives, and foreign exchange contracts **on behalf of another person** for both retail and wholesale clients

> **General advice only:** Kohle Capital Markets Pty Ltd is authorised to provide **general financial product advice** only. This means advice is not tailored to any individual's personal objectives, financial situation, or needs. It does not constitute personal advice or a recommendation to invest.

A copy of the licence is available on the [Regulatory Documents]({{ '/regulatory-documents.html' | relative_url }}) page.

## 4. Key Regulatory Conditions

The AFSL is subject to conditions prescribed by ASIC under the *Corporations Act 2001*, including but not limited to:

- **Key Person Requirement:** Compliance functions require a designated key person (Christopher Mark Gore). ASIC must be notified within 5 business days of any changes to this key person.
- **Financial Requirements:** The licensee must at all times meet base-level financial requirements, including the ability to pay all debts as they fall due and satisfy applicable adjusted surplus liquid funds (ASLF) thresholds.
- **Compliance Measures:** The licensee must establish and maintain compliance measures ensuring adherence to financial services laws.
- **Training:** All natural persons providing financial product advice to retail clients must meet ASIC-approved training and competency requirements.
- **Audit Requirements:** The licensee must lodge an annual audit opinion by a registered company auditor with ASIC.
- **External Dispute Resolution:** The licensee must maintain membership of the **Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)**.
- **MDA Prohibition:** The licensee must not provide a Managed Discretionary Account (MDA) service to retail clients except when operating a registered scheme.
- **Record Retention:** Financial Services Guides and records of advice must be retained for a minimum of 7 years.
