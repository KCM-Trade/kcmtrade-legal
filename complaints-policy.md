# Complaints Policy

**Effective Date:** 27 January 2026  
**Last Updated:** 27 January 2026

This Complaints Policy (the “Policy”) sets out the procedures adopted by **Kohle Capital Markets Limited** (the “Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) for the receipt, handling, investigation, and resolution of complaints in respect of digital services, platforms, and applications operated under the **KCM Trade** brand (the “Services”).

This Policy forms part of the Company’s legal and compliance framework and is intended to meet applicable legal, regulatory, and platform governance requirements.

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Policy is to:

- Provide a clear and accessible mechanism for the submission of complaints;  
- Ensure complaints are addressed in a fair, objective, and timely manner;  
- Enhance transparency and accountability in complaint handling; and  
- Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and platform obligations.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all complaints submitted by users or prospective users of the Services, including complaints relating to:

- Account access and administration;  
- Trading execution or transaction issues;  
- Platform functionality or availability;  
- Customer support interactions;  
- Data protection, privacy, or security matters; and  
- Compliance with the Company’s legal and regulatory obligations.

---

## 3. Definition of Complaint

For the purposes of this Policy, a “Complaint” means any expression of dissatisfaction, whether oral or written, relating to the provision of the Services, the Company’s conduct, or any related documentation or interaction.

---

---

## 5. Acknowledgement and Handling

Upon receipt of a Complaint, the Company will:

- Acknowledge receipt within a reasonable timeframe;  
- Record the Complaint in its internal complaints register; and  
- Conduct an impartial investigation, which may involve consultation with relevant internal departments, review of records, and requests for additional information where necessary.

The Company aims to respond with a resolution or substantive update within a reasonable period after investigation.

---

## 6. Resolution and Response

The Company will communicate the outcome of its investigation to the complainant. This communication will include:

- The findings of the investigation;  
- Any actions taken or proposed remedial measures;  
- Information on further escalation rights, if applicable.

The Company’s response does not constitute an admission of liability.

---
