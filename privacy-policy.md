## 4. Personal Data We Collect

### 4.1 Data Provided During Registration

**Mandatory Information:**
- Email address  
- Full legal name  
- Mobile phone number (with country code)  
- Date of birth  
- Country of residence  
- Password (hashed and encrypted)

**Optional Information:**
- Title  
- Introducing Broker (IB) or referral code  
- Preferred language  

---

### 4.2 Data Collected During App Usage

- Trading account identifiers (MT4 / MT5)
- Trading history, orders, positions, and transactions
- Account configuration, leverage, and margin parameters
- Deposit and withdrawal information
- Bank account or payment method details
- Uploaded documents (identity documents, proof of address, KYC/AML materials)
- Customer support communications (chat, tickets, emails)
- In-app messages and system notifications
- User preferences and application settings

---

### 4.3 Data Collected Automatically

**Device & Technical Data**
- Device model and manufacturer
- Operating system, app version, and build number
- Device identifiers used for push notifications
- Screen resolution and device type

**Usage & Network Data**
- IP address
- Login timestamps and session duration
- Feature usage and interaction data
- Error logs, crash reports, and diagnostic data

**Location Data**
- Country and regional location detection
- Optional GPS location during registration strictly to verify service availability
