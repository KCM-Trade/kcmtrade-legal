## 1. What is Copy Trading?

Copy trading is a feature that allows users ("Copiers") to automatically replicate, in real time, the trading activity of other users or strategy providers ("Strategy Providers"). When a Strategy Provider opens, modifies, or closes a trade, a proportionate equivalent trade is automatically executed in the Copier's account.

The Copy Trading Service is provided through **Pelican Exchange Limited** (Company No. 09437275, registered at 78 York Street, London, England), which operates the social trading platform at [copy-trade.io](https://copy-trade.io/). ## 4. Copy Trading Risks

> **Copy trading involves a high degree of risk. Users should read the following risk disclosures carefully before using the Copy Trading Service.**

### 4.1 Leverage and Loss Risk

Copy trades are leveraged in the same manner as directly placed trades. Losses may equal or exceed the amount allocated to copying a Strategy Provider. For further information on leverage and margin risks, please read the [Leverage and Margin Policy]({{ '/leverage-margin-policy.html' | relative_url }}) and the [Risk Disclosure Statement]({{ '/risk-disclosure.html' | relative_url }}).

### 4.2 Proportional Allocation Risk

Copy trades are executed on a proportional basis relative to the Strategy Provider's position size and the Copier's allocated funds. Due to differences in account size and timing of trade execution, the proportion of the Copier's account at risk on any given trade may differ from the proportion at risk in the Strategy Provider's account.

### 4.3 Execution Timing Risk

Copy trades may not be executed at exactly the same time as the corresponding Strategy Provider trade, due to system latency, processing delays, or order queue management. This may result in the Copier entering or exiting a position at a different price from the Strategy Provider, which may affect the profitability of the copied trade.

### 4.4 Strategy Change Risk

A Strategy Provider may change their trading approach, increase risk levels, reduce activity, or close their strategy at any time. The Copier may not be aware of such changes in advance and may be exposed to unintended risks as a result.

### 4.5 Strategy Provider Account Closure Risk

If a Strategy Provider closes their account or ceases to be available on the platform, copying will be discontinued. Any open positions that were copied from that Strategy Provider will remain open in the Copier's account until the Copier manually closes them, unless the platform automatically closes such positions in accordance with applicable settings.

### 4.6 Simultaneous Multiple Strategies Risk

Where a Copier copies multiple Strategy Providers simultaneously, the aggregate risk exposure across all copied strategies may be correlated, including where different strategies trade the same or related instruments, potentially leading to concentrated losses.

### 4.7 Risk of Total Loss

It is possible for a Copier to lose the entire amount allocated to copying one or more Strategy Providers. Users should only allocate to the Copy Trading Service funds they are prepared to lose in full.

### 4.8 Technology Risk

The Copy Trading Service is dependent on third-party technology infrastructure provided by **Pelican Exchange Limited** (copy-trade.io). System failures, connectivity interruptions, platform outages, or software errors may affect the ability to execute, monitor, or close copy trades. Pelican Exchange Limited expressly disclaims warranties of uninterrupted or error-free operation of its platform under its EULA.
